The work

Show up with purpose.
Give people a reason to engage.

Bring curiosity, follow-through, and ideas of your own. The aim is a page that reflects Thomas Fence and takes an active interest in the people and businesses around it.

01 / PUBLISH

Keep the page moving.

  • Publish and schedule pre-written posts with care.
  • Check the wording, links, and visuals before a post goes out.
  • Bring forward ideas that make the next post more useful or engaging.
02 / CONNECT

Be part of the conversation.

  • Read local businesses’ posts and leave thoughtful comments.
  • Respond to conversations on the Thomas Fence page.
  • Build familiarity with complementary businesses, community groups, and local organizations.
03 / SHARE

Notice what is happening.

  • Find a recent, relevant news article from a page Thomas Fence follows.
  • Share it with a short explanation of why it matters to the local community.
  • Credit the original source and check the date before sharing.
04 / CREATE

Bring the work to life.

  • Use Thomas Fence’s YouTube videos when they fit the conversation.
  • Suggest new ways to introduce the company’s work and connect with local businesses.
  • Use AI to explore wording, then edit for accuracy, personality, and a natural voice.

A practical daily rhythm

One focused hour.
Thoughtful choices throughout.

Balance the hour around the day’s priorities. A worthwhile conversation deserves attention. An irrelevant post can wait.

01

Check in.

Read comments and recent activity on the Thomas Fence page. Notice what people are asking.

02

Publish with care.

Prepare the approved post or check the scheduled queue. Match each post with the right visual or video.

03

Read locally.

Visit relevant Northwest Arkansas pages. Look for useful news, community projects, and conversations.

04

Add something.

Comment when there is something specific to say. Ask a thoughtful question or recognize work worth noticing.

05

Share with context.

Choose a relevant recent article and explain its local connection. Keep the original source visible.

06

Keep learning.

Leave a short record of useful conversations, responses, and an idea worth trying next.

The voice

A person behind the page.
You can hear the difference.

Warm, specific, and interested. The writing reflects someone who read the post and cares about what is happening here.

  • Read the whole post before responding.
  • Add a relevant thought or question.
  • Keep the language natural and easy to read.
  • Edit AI drafts. Check every factual claim.
  • Let the conversation matter on its own.
Illustrative example · not a real post
A local business shares“We’re opening a new workshop in Springdale and hosting an open house for our neighbors.”
A thoughtful replyA chance to see what gets made here in Springdale is worth a visit. Will there be a workshop tour during the open house?
Specific to the post. Interested in the business. An invitation to keep talking.

The local network

Know the neighborhood.
Look beyond the fence line.

Build a thoughtful working list of 20 Northwest Arkansas business and community pages. Focus on organizations with a local connection and businesses that do not compete directly with Thomas Fence.

  • Chambers & communityLocal business news, openings, neighborhood projects, and opportunities to participate.
  • Entrepreneurs & new ideasStartup Junkie, Ignite, Fuel, and One Million Cups are starting points for research.
  • Complementary businessesBuilders, landscapers, property professionals, and others whose work connects with the places Thomas Fence serves.
  • Local stories worth sharingUse Threadlines NWA as a discovery source, then check each organization and the original story.

The final 20-page list is a separate research item. These are research directions, not a verified list of LinkedIn pages or existing partnerships.

Who this fits

Passion. Presence. Persistence.
And ideas of your own.

THE PERSON

Interested enough to notice.

  • Enjoys social media and building business relationships.
  • Writes clearly and recognizes when a sentence sounds stiff or generic.
  • Brings fresh ideas and explains why they fit Thomas Fence.
  • Works consistently and takes care with the company’s reputation.
  • Uses AI thoughtfully without giving up their own judgment.
THE PROGRESS

Show what is connecting.

  • Keep a reliable posting rhythm.
  • Point to real conversations and repeat engagement from relevant local businesses.
  • Review available page analytics alongside the work published.
  • Explain what earned a response and what to try next.
  • Build a more recognizable, useful local presence over time.
